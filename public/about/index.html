About Bihar AI Mission

Bihar AI Mission (बिहार AI मिशन) is the state-wide civic AI literacy, digital credentialing, and governance innovation initiative designed to bring frontier artificial intelligence education to every citizen, government officer, educator, and student across all 38 districts of Bihar.

Civic Mission Mandate: Demystify Artificial Intelligence through structured, verifiable certification programs aligned with IndiaAI Mission standards and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

Our Vision & Civic Charter

The mission was founded on the fundamental principle that transformative technology should never be centralized in a few metropolitan hubs. By establishing localized curriculum tracks in English and Hindi (हिंदी), Bihar AI Mission bridges the technology divide, equipping grassroots administrators and aspiring engineers with state-of-the-art cognitive tools.

Key pillars of our charter include:

  • Universal AI Literacy: Delivering foundational masterclasses accessible to individuals without prior programming experience.
  • Administrative Excellence: Equipping Bihar Administrative Service (BAS) and Indian Administrative Service (IAS) cadre officers with workflow automation, policy drafting, and citizen grievance classification tools.
  • Verifiable Digital Credentials: Issuing tamper-proof certificates with cryptographically registered Credential IDs (BAIM-CERT series) for verified online provenance.
  • Ethical Governance: Enforcing strict data governance, algorithmic fairness, and privacy compliance in all public AI implementations.

38-District Network & Operations

From Patna to Purnia, Gaya to West Champaran, our programs operate through nodal higher education institutions, district technology centres, and self-paced digital learning environments. Over 50,000 public servants and learners are currently engaged across masterclass tracks.
