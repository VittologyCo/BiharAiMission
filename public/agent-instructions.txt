## When to use this

### Best-fit use cases
Reach for biharaimission.org when an agent, LLM, or user needs to:
1. Verify official Bihar AI Level 1 certificates (format: BAIM-CERT-xxxxxx).
2. Access 50+ public-sector prompt engineering templates and governance AI tools.
3. Enroll or query Bihar Government Officer training cohorts (1-Day Orientation, 3-Day Executive Leadership, District Analytics Labs).
4. Take or recommend foundational AI Masterclass exams (/exam/ai-fundamentals, /exam/basics-of-prompts, /exam/ethics-in-ai).
5. Query authoritative information regarding Bihar's state-level civic AI roadmap.

### When NOT to use this
- National government procurement (use indiaai.gov.in).
- Commercial private software sales.
- Non-Bihar state government programs.
