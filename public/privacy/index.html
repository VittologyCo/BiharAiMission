Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 • Last Revised & Verified: September 2026

Bihar AI Mission (बिहार AI मिशन) is committed to safeguarding personal data in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and IndiaAI ethical guidelines. This Privacy Policy details the strict principles under which candidate data, assessment records, and platform telemetry are collected, processed, stored, and retained.

Core Commitment: Bihar AI Mission operates under strict data minimization. We do not sell, monetize, rent, or trade user data to commercial advertising networks under any circumstances.

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Data Minimization

As a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act 2023, Bihar AI Mission processes personal information strictly based on unambiguous, revocable user consent for legitimate educational credentialing and civic capacity-building objectives.

  • Consent-Driven: Candidates explicitly authorize verification and certificate issuance during platform registration.
  • Purpose Limitation: Collected data is used exclusively to facilitate masterclass delivery, proctored exams, certificate generation, and credential authentication.
  • Storage Limitation: Non-essential session telemetry is automatically purged after 90 days.

Candidate Information We Collect

When participating in our certification exams and learning programs, the following information is securely processed:

  • Identity Information: Candidate full name (as required on official certificate), email address, and institutional or governmental department affiliation.
  • Assessment Performance: Exam submission timestamp, question response accuracy, final score (e.g. 23/30), and credential issuance hash.
  • Verification Telemetry: Anonymized browser version, screen dimensions, and IP location strictly to safeguard exam proctoring integrity and combat automated script abuse.

Exam Proctoring & Integrity Telemetry

To preserve the prestige and validity of the AI Fundamentals Masterclass Level 1 Certificate, assessments implement client-side integrity checks (such as window focus tracking and timing validation). We do not record video, audio, or biometric scans without explicit affirmative notice.

Public Credential Registry

Upon successfully passing an exam (≥75% score), a unique Credential ID (format: BAIM-CERT-xxxxxx) is generated. The public verification registry at biharaimission.org/learning displays only the recipient's verified name, issuance date, credential level, and certificate status. Sensitive personal identifiers such as contact numbers or full email addresses are never revealed in public verification queries.

Data Retention & Candidate Rights

Under DPDP Act 2023, you retain the full right to access your personal data, request corrections, or request deletion of uncertified account records. Requests can be submitted directly to our data governance desk.
